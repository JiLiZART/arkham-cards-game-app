## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The Forgotten Age content

The content SHALL hold The Forgotten Age as a hand-written campaign under `content/campaigns/the-forgotten-age/` (`generated: false`) with its prologue and supply point, the scenarios in play order (The Untamed Wilds, The Doom of Eztli, Threads of Fate, The Boundary Beyond, Heart of the Elders Part 1, Heart of the Elders Part 2, The City of Archives, The Depths of Yoth, Shattered Aeons), Interludes I–V, the epilogue, and Turn Back Time reached from the epilogue. Each scenario SHALL set up as the campaign guide prints (its exploration deck, every copy of a set-aside card, random picks, the conditional encounter sets, the starts by log and by choice), carry the chaos token effects of all four difficulties from its printed scenario card, connect its locations as the printed connection symbols and the guide's placement maps say, have a scenario script, and end in each resolution the guide names. The log entries the guide records from the table (Yig's Fury, the Harbinger's damage, the jungle watches, the depth reached, the tasks completed, the investigators killed) SHALL come from the scenario, not from a question.

#### Scenario: The Untamed Wilds sets up

- **WHEN** The Untamed Wilds starts with two investigators
- **THEN** only the Expedition Camp is in play with everyone there, the exploration deck holds the five Jungle locations and one each of Lost in the Wilds, Overgrowth, Snake Bite, Low on Supplies and Arrows from the Trees, the three Ruins locations and the Agents of Yig set are set aside, four Poisoned are set aside, and no exploration card is in the encounter deck

#### Scenario: Exploring the jungle

- **GIVEN** Ursula at the Expedition Camp during The Untamed Wilds
- **WHEN** she uses the agenda's Explore and draws a Jungle location connected to the camp
- **THEN** it is in play with its clues and she is on it; a tablet revealed afterwards reads −X with X the cards left in the exploration deck, at most 5

#### Scenario: The Doom of Eztli replayed

- **GIVEN** The Doom of Eztli reached Resolution 4 once before
- **WHEN** it is set up again
- **THEN** one doom is on the Entryway, and Resolution 4 again would place two next time

#### Scenario: Threads of Fate with three act decks

- **GIVEN** the log records that Alejandro has the relic and the players chose to go to the police
- **WHEN** Threads of Fate starts
- **THEN** three act decks are in play, each at its first act of the version the log and the choice picked, the Northside, Downtown, Easttown, Miskatonic University, Rivertown, Curiositie Shoppe and Velma's Diner are in play with Town Hall set aside, everyone starts at Rivertown, and only two False Lead, three Hunting Shadow and no Midnight Masks act or agenda are in the encounter deck

#### Scenario: The Boundary Beyond's start and sets

- **GIVEN** a campaign bag with two cultist tokens
- **WHEN** The Boundary Beyond starts
- **THEN** the players choose to start at the Zócalo or Coyoacán, the Pnakotic Brotherhood and Dark Cult sets are in the encounter deck and Yig's Venom and Guardians of Time are not, and a Tenochtitlán location explored into takes the place of the Present-Day location with its symbol

#### Scenario: Heart of the Elders Part 1 starts at the Mouth

- **GIVEN** the log records two paths known
- **WHEN** Heart of the Elders Part 1 starts
- **THEN** Mouth of K'n-yan (Cavern's Maw) is the only location in play with two pillar tokens, everyone is there, one of the three Ruins locations is in the exploration deck and the other two removed

#### Scenario: Heart of the Elders Part 2 is one Mouth

- **WHEN** Heart of the Elders Part 2 starts
- **THEN** one Mouth of K'n-yan is in play, Depths Beneath side up, the vengeance cards recorded as the jungle watches are in the victory display, and the Descent to Yoth is set aside

#### Scenario: The City of Archives by the log

- **GIVEN** the log records that the investigators resisted
- **WHEN** The City of Archives starts
- **THEN** the Restraining Chamber is the start with the other two Interview Rooms in play at random, the Yithian Observer is in the victory display, every unique Item asset and Alejandro Vela are set aside, and each investigator plays as Body of a Yithian with their own deck

#### Scenario: The Depths of Yoth by Yig's Fury

- **GIVEN** Yig's Fury at 12
- **WHEN** The Depths of Yoth starts
- **THEN** the agenda deck starts at agenda 3a, one random Yoth location other than Steps of Yoth is in play as the start, the depth level is 1, the exploration deck is Steps of Yoth plus four random others, and the Harbinger is set aside with its recorded damage

#### Scenario: Shattered Aeons by the tally

- **GIVEN** Yig's Fury at 23
- **WHEN** Shattered Aeons starts
- **THEN** two encounter cards are face down in the victory display, each counting one vengeance, the exploration deck holds the three Otherworld locations with Wracked by Time, Between Worlds and Ancient Evils, and the Dark Cult set is gathered when cultist tokens outnumber tablet tokens in the bag

#### Scenario: Turn Back Time after the epilogue

- **GIVEN** Shattered Aeons ended in Resolution 4 and the epilogue was read
- **WHEN** the campaign continues
- **THEN** Turn Back Time starts with Eztli's agendas and the acts 04345–04347, Relic of Ages "…A Device, of Some Sort" out of the game and the owner's "Repossess the Past" set aside, an extra elder thing token in the bag, and the log's entries after Scenario I crossed off
