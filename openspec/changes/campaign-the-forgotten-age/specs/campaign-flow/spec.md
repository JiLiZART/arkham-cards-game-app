## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The Forgotten Age interludes and supply points

The campaign flow of The Forgotten Age SHALL run the prologue's expedition leader choice and Supply Point 1 (points 10, 7, 5, 4 by player count; the eleven supplies at their printed prices), the Resupply Point after Threads of Fate (8, 5, 4, 3; ten supplies; 3 experience to remove a Poisoned, 5 to remove one trauma, once each), and Interludes I–V with their supply checks and cross-offs (provisions per investigator with Low on Rations per missing one; gasoline from any one investigator else Out of Gas; medicine per investigator to remove a Poisoned else The Poison Spreads; the lookout's binoculars; the blanket; the map, canteen, chalk, torches and braziers branches) as engine steps, not as unsupported text. Yig's Fury SHALL be counted from each scenario's vengeance; the Harbinger's damage SHALL come from the scenario; Interlude IV's mishaps SHALL draw one or two chaos tokens from the campaign bag and resolve the printed outcome; the epilogue SHALL lead to Turn Back Time after Resolution 4 of Shattered Aeons.

#### Scenario: Interlude I with a lookout

- **GIVEN** Interlude I after The Untamed Wilds with Ursula holding binoculars
- **WHEN** the lead chooses Ursula as the lookout
- **THEN** the flow reads the binoculars branch and the camp is not surprised; had nobody binoculars, the other branch is read

#### Scenario: Resupply after Threads of Fate

- **GIVEN** Leo with 7 experience and a Poisoned in his deck
- **WHEN** the Resupply Point runs
- **THEN** he may pay 3 experience to remove the Poisoned, then buys supplies with the points of his player count, and his experience is 4 less

#### Scenario: Interlude IV's mishap

- **WHEN** Interlude IV asks each investigator to reveal a chaos token
- **THEN** a token is drawn from the campaign bag for each, its printed mishap resolved (trauma, a Poisoned, or nothing), and the bag is unchanged after
