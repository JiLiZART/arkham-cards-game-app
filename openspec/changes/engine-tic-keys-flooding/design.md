## Context

The engine keeps every fact about a card in a component table of `World.tables` (`src/engine/world/types.ts` `CardTables`: zone, owner, clues, doom, location, attachedTo…; ADR-0024) and every fact about the scenario in a named flag family (`src/engine/world/flags.ts`: `connections`, `underAct`, `atTurnEnd`…). A scenario reaches the campaign only through notes it leaves in `flags` (`log:<section>:<id>`, `count:<section>:<id>`) that `campaignNotes()` lists and `applyScenarioEnd` (`src/engine/campaign/campaign.ts:161`) records; the only permanent bag change is the setup op `addChaosToken`, read back at the scenario's end. Token X sources are a closed enum on `TokenEffect.x` (`src/content/schema.ts:1311`) computed by `tokenX` (`src/engine/rules/tokens.ts:9`). Per-turn counters per investigator (`actionsTaken`, `defeatedHealthThisTurn`) are reset in `nextTurnHandler` (`src/engine/rules/round.ts:145`); `endTurnHandler` (`round.ts:362`) runs `atTurnEnd` effects. An investigator enters a location through `arriveHandler` (`src/engine/rules/investigators.ts:450`), which opens the `enterLocation` window; `revealLocation` (`investigators.ts:479`) places clues; `defeatedHandler` (`investigators.ts:350`) eliminates. Enemies leave play through `defeatEnemyHandler` (`src/engine/rules/enemies.ts:352`) and through `moveCard` (`src/engine/movement.ts:81`) when an effect discards or removes them. Fast options during a turn come from `legalTurnActions` (`round.ts:276`).

Nothing in the engine, the schema or the UI knows keys, flood levels or a story shown mid-scenario (ChaosToken has no key colours; `CHOICE` has no story kind; `ModFlag` has no flood caps). The card texts this change must carry: "Randomly choose 1 of the set-aside facedown keys and place it on Unfamiliar Chamber without looking at it" (07047), "If investigators at Altar to Dagon control the blue key or three or more other keys … Take control of the set-aside green key" (07050), "Tidal Pool gets +1 shroud while it is partially flooded and +2 shroud while it is fully flooded" (07103), "After you successfully evade The Amalgam by 2 or more: Take control of 1 key on it" (07053), "Forced – When your turn begins, if you are at a fully flooded location, you struggle for air: When your turn ends, if you did not enter an unflooded or partially flooded location during your turn, take 5 direct damage" (07042), "You ignore the Forced ability on the current agenda" (07338), "While you are in this vehicle, treat your location as if it were unflooded" (07178), "−1 for each key on this card" (07274), "Read Flashback XIV in the Campaign Guide" (07328).

In-force ADRs read: 0004 (keys, flood and struggle are game state; which key a player looks at is view state), 0011 (the flow stays the only interpreter of campaign steps: a flashback leaves notes, the flow records them), 0013 (a card works when its lines are accounted for: the new vocabulary gives the lines abilities, two new line rules cover "cannot be flooded" and "enters play fully flooded"), 0015 (the Grimoire's _Keys_ and _Flood_ entries are the rules text), 0021–0024 (two new tables, JSON-plain), 0025 (one-way imports: tables and flags in `world/`, ops in `effects/`, rules in `rules/`), 0026 (`KEY` and `FLOOD` as constant objects), 0033 (struggle-for-air and the flashback are step kinds), 0034 (scripts get verbs `placeKey`, `takeKey`, `flashback` and reads `keysOf`, `keyHolder`, `floodOf`).

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** the key and flood vocabulary every TIC scenario's cards and reference cards need, as data where the cards are data and as `api.ts` reads and verbs where they are scripts; the Grimoire's key rules (claim, drop, give, transfer on leaving play) in the engine once, not in each scenario; struggle for air once, in the round; a scenario able to tell a story mid-scenario and change the campaign's bag at the scenario's end; the flood table shaped so `engine-tdc-flood-levels-tasks` adds TDC's ops and conditions without a second table.

**Non-Goals:** bless/curse (`engine-bless-curse`); grid placement and "adjacent means connected" (`engine-location-instances-grid`); location decks, vehicles, the depths and other out-of-play zones, slumbering/immune enemies, conditional token modifiers `instead if …` and the `agendaStage`/`locationsEastOfYou`/`barriersAroundYou` X sources (`engine-scenario-structure`); barriers (In Too Deep's own script); captured investigators (A Light in the Fog's script); the scenario scripts and `cards.json` entries of the cycle (`campaign-the-innsmouth-conspiracy`); the key-bearing _cards_ of The Scarlet Keys (`engine-tsk-keys-concealed` decides whether its keys are rows of this table with a card holder or cards of their own); TDC's tasks.

## Decisions

1. **Keys are one table keyed by colour, not seven card instances.** `World.tables.keys: Record<KeyColor, { holder: InstId | InvId | "aside" | "removed"; faceup: boolean }>`, created by the setup op `placeKeys { aside?: { faceup: KeyColor[]; facedown: KeyColor[] }; removed?: KeyColor[]; on?: { code: string; colors: KeyColor[] }[] }` (a scenario without the op has no keys). Holders are instance ids (a location, enemy, asset, act, agenda or the scenario card) or investigator ids; the store's `withCopiedTables` covers the new table, so the state stays JSON-plain and copies stay cheap (ADR-0021/0023). _Why:_ a key has no card, no zone, no text; it is a token with a position, and every question the cards ask ("keys on this card", "keys you control", "the blue key") is a lookup by colour or by holder. _Alternative:_ a `CardInst` per key in an attachment zone — rejected: every card query, the piles, the views and the UI would have to exclude them; and "randomly choose 1 of the set-aside facedown keys" is a pick among table rows, not a deck draw.

2. **The Grimoire's key rules live in the rules, not in cards.** `legalTurnActions` adds a fast option "Take the <colour> key" for each key on the investigator's location when the location has no clues (and `takeKey` is not forbidden by a scenario flag `keysClaimedOnlyBy` that Devil Reef sets for its Y'ha-nthlei locations), and "Give the <colour> key to <investigator>" for each key held and each other investigator at the same location. `defeatEnemyHandler` and the evade success stage move every key on the enemy to `step.by` (the investigator who defeated or evaded it), else, when the enemy leaves play by any other path (`moveCard` to a non-play zone), to its location. `defeatedHandler` moves an eliminated investigator's keys to their location. A key taken facedown turns faceup. Resigning does not drop keys by itself (Sealed Exit says so explicitly; the resolution reads keys the investigators still control). _Why:_ one place per rule, tested once; a scenario script that wants different behaviour sets a flag, as `noReady` and `peril` already do. _Alternative:_ each scenario's script handling defeat and elimination — rejected: eight scenarios, one rule.

3. **Key effects and conditions as data.** Effects in `effects/placement.ts`: `placeKey { color: KeyColor | "randomFacedown" | "randomAside"; on: "self" | "yourLocation" | { code } }` (the random ones draw with `randInt` so replays agree), `takeKey { color?: KeyColor; from: "self" | "yourLocation" | "eventEnemy"; count?: number }` (choose when several), `giveKey`, `spendKey { colors?: KeyColor[]; count?: number; to?: "scenarioCard" | "removed" }` (Lair of Dagon puts spent keys on the scenario card), `placeYourKeys { on: "self" | "yourLocation" | "eventEnemy" }`. Conditions in `queries.ts`: `youControlKey { color?: KeyColor; min?: number }`, `investigatorsControlKeys { min; at?: "yourLocation" }`, `keyOn { card: "self" | "yourLocation" | "act" | "scenarioCard"; color?: KeyColor; min?: number }`, `keysAtYourLocation { min }`. Costs: `Cost.spendKey` (Lair's Oaths and Hall of the Deep: "spend the red key"). Token X: `keysInvestigatorsControl`, `keysOnScenarioCard` in `tokenX`. A resolution effect `recordKey { color: KeyColor; on: "act" | "investigators" | "scenarioCard"; key: string }` writes `log:<key>` when the key is there at the end (Devil Reef's "the key on the current act", A Light in the Fog's black and red keys), so Interlude III's branches read plain `logHas`. The schema gains `KeyColor = z.enum(KEY)`.

4. **Flood is a card table with three levels and two caps.** `CardTables.flood: Record<InstId, 0 | 1 | 2>` (`FLOOD.None | Partial | Full`; absent = unflooded). `LocationDef.flood?: "partial" | "full"` sets the level when the card enters play (revealed or not: "Y'ha-nthlei Sanctum enters play fully flooded (even while it is unrevealed)"). Effects `increaseFlood { at: "yourLocation" | "self" | "eachRevealed" | { filter } }`, `decreaseFlood`, `setFlood { level }`; the caps are `ModFlag`s `cannotBeFlooded` and `cannotBeFullyFlooded` (constant modifiers with `scope: "self"`, as `damageCapOne`), read by the three ops so an increase on a capped location does nothing and the "if you cannot, take 1 horror" branch of A Light in the Fog's cultist can test `locationFullyFlooded` before and after. Conditions `locationFlooded { at }`, `locationFullyFlooded { at }`, `unfloodedLocations { min; trait? }`. The investigator `ModFlag` `treatLocationUnflooded` makes `floodOf(game, loc, inv)` answer 0 for that investigator (Fishing Vessel; the vehicle change sets the modifier while the investigator is aboard). _Why:_ the questions are "what level is this location at, for this investigator"; a number in a table answers both the token lines and the shroud modifiers through the existing constant-modifier `when` conditions. `engine-tdc-flood-levels-tasks` adds its ops to the same table; the level enum is not TIC's to extend.

5. **Struggle for air is a round rule with an agenda flag.** The agenda's constant modifier `{ flag: "struggleForAir", scope: "scenario" }` (07042–07044, 07164–07166, 07275–07279, 07312–07314 all print the same forced ability) turns it on. `nextTurnHandler` sets `struggling(inv)` when the investigator's location is fully flooded for them and the flag is on; `arriveHandler` appends the location to `enteredThisTurn(inv)`; `endTurnHandler` pushes `STEP.StruggleForAir { inv }`, whose handler deals 5 direct damage when `struggling` is set, no entry in `enteredThisTurn` is below Full for the investigator, and the investigator has no `ignoreAgendaForced` (Diving Suit, a constant modifier on its bearer). Both flags reset with the turn (ADR-0033: a step kind, not a closure). _Alternative:_ `atTurnEnd` effects queued by each agenda's script — rejected: the flag is one line on twelve agendas and the rule is tested once.

6. **A flashback is a step that asks a story prompt and leaves notes.** `STEP.Flashback { id: string; tokens?: ChaosToken[]; then?: Effect[] }` (verb `flashback(game, id, opts)` in `api.ts`; card data `{ op: "flashback", id, tokens? }`). Its handler asks a `CHOICE.Story` prompt with `promptKey: "flashback.<campaignId>.<id>"` (the text is the guide's, under `content/i18n/<locale>/campaign-texts/<campaign>.json`, as resolutions are) and one option per token to remove when `tokens` is given, else "Continue"; on the answer it sets `log:memories_recovered:<id>` and, for a removed token, `bag:remove:<token>` (a count, so two removals of a skull add up); `bag:add:<token>` is a plain effect `addCampaignToken { token }` for Flashback VII's "+1". `applyScenarioEnd` applies the bag notes after the log notes: `push` for adds, `splice` of the first match for removes (ignored when the campaign bag has none, as the guide's "if able"). The same channel carries bless/curse adds once `engine-bless-curse` lands (Lair of Dagon's elder thing adds a curse to the _scenario's_ bag, which is `addChaosToken` in play; the campaign bag is only touched by notes). _Why:_ the campaign state is not in the game state; the only honest time to change it is when the scenario's state is handed back (ADR-0061). _Alternative:_ a `campaignBag` mirror inside `GameState` edited in play — rejected: two copies of one bag, and a restarted scenario (ENGINE_VERSION bump, abandon) would carry edits the campaign never saw.

7. **Line rules in `implemented.ts`** for the two lines the data expresses without an ability: `^<name> cannot be flooded\.$` (true when the card has the `cannotBeFlooded` modifier) and `^<name> enters play (fully|partially) flooded( \(even while it is unrevealed\))?\.$` (true when `LocationDef.flood` is set). "Keys" lines are abilities, so no rule.

8. **Views and UI.** `CardView.keys: KeyColor[]` and `InvView.keys` are derived in `viewOf`/`invView` from the table (ADR-0022, 0036: the UI reads views, never `tables.keys`). `scene/CardMesh` draws up to seven small discs on a card's lower edge, facedown ones grey with a "?"; `hud/Investigators` shows held keys beside the resources; a location's flood level is a badge with the `flood.partial` / `flood.full` string and a blue tint on the card frame. The story prompt reuses the resolution text panel in `hud/Modals` with the options as buttons. Strings `key.red` … `key.white`, `flood.partial`, `flood.full`, `action.takeKey`, `action.giveKey`, `prompt.flashback`, `prompt.flashback.removeToken` in `ui.json`, en and ru.

9. **`ENGINE_VERSION` bump.** Two new tables and a step kind; a saved scenario restarts with its campaign kept, as every rules change.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [The fast "Take key" option appears on every location with keys and no clues, every turn] → it is listed with the other fast options, not a prompt; the bot ignores it; a test asserts a location with clues offers none.
- [A key on a card that is set aside, shuffled into a deck or flipped] → `moveCard` out of play drops keys on the card's location when it has one (enemies, attachments), else sets them aside; a flipped double-sided card keeps its instance id and so its keys (engine-scenario-structure's flip keeps ids).
- [Flood levels for a location the investigator "treats as unflooded" differ per reader] → `floodOf(game, loc, inv?)` takes the reader; the token lines and shroud modifiers pass the tested investigator, the UI badge shows the table's level.
- [The flashback prompt mid-scenario is a new choice kind for the UI and the bot] → the bot answers "Continue" or the first token (as it does for `generic`); online play forwards it as any choice.
- [TDC extends the flood table after this lands] → the enum and ops are named for the rule, not the cycle; `engine-tdc-flood-levels-tasks` adds TDC's ops beside them and must not fork the table (stated in its proposal's dependencies).
- [The Scarlet Keys' keys are cards with their own text] → not modelled here; `engine-tsk-keys-concealed` may hold a TSK key as a row whose `holder` is a card _and_ a card of its own; the colour enum is TIC's seven and TSK's names would be a second constant set.

## Migration Plan

Engine, schema, strings and UI in one change; `ENGINE_VERSION` bump (saved scenarios restart, campaigns kept). Rollback: revert; the tables are additive and the setup op is used by no shipped scenario until `campaign-the-innsmouth-conspiracy`.
