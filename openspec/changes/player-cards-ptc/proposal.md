## Why

The Path to Carcosa cycle's player cards are in the deck builder but not in the game: of the 140 player cards of the ptc, eotp, tuo, apot, tpm, bsr, dca and rtptc packs, 4 work (Overzealous, Drawing the Sign, Cherished Keepsake, First Aid), and the six Carcosa investigators (Mark Harrigan, Minh Thi Phan, Sefina Rousseau, Akachi Onyele, William Yorick, Lola Hayes) have no behaviour, so a Carcosa campaign is played with core-set decks only. Coverage reports ptc 2 of 47, eotp 1 of 17, tuo 0 of 13, apot 0 of 12, tpm 1 of 12, bsr 0 of 11, dca 0 of 13, rtptc 0 of 15 (report `ptc.md`, section 5).

## Decided (assumed)

- Story cards (15 codes above) are the campaign changes' and are listed here only as done elsewhere; no card is written twice.
- Investigator abilities are declared in `cards.json` under the investigator's code, as the core and Dunwich investigators are.
- Per-pack task groups in cycle order, so a pack can land on its own and tick its program row.
- **Time Warp reuses the existing Undo (user decision).** The store already takes back choices by replaying the recorded game without them (`src/ui/state/store.ts`: `runUndo`, `testStartIndex`). Time Warp uses the same replay rather than a copy of the state: the engine notes the choice count when an action starts, and playing Time Warp asks for a rewind to that count. `replay` in `src/engine/index.ts` resolves the rewind, so a saved game, a replay and every peer online reach the same state from the same list of choices.
- **Charon's Obol's kill (user decision).** The investigator is killed when the scenario ends with them defeated, even when the resolution would otherwise save them.
- **Lola's role** is investigator state (`role`), set by a prompt after the opening hand, read by `canPlay`, commit and ability checks as a class filter (Neutral always allowed); Improvisation and Crisis of Identity switch it; the once-per-round fast switch is a limit flag.
- The parallel investigators and Enthralling Encore's player cards (90088–90097) are `player-cards-investigator-decks`', not here and not `player-cards-side-and-promo`'s.
