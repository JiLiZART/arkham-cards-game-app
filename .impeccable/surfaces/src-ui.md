# Arkham Noir (whole app)

Scope: every surface: title, setup wizard, campaign log, card catalog, settings, how-to, in-game HUD and table. Visitor mode: Operate (table, HUD, wizard, catalog); the title screen leans Persuade.

Audience and job: hot-seat groups and solo players (Russian first) playing long campaigns on one screen. Tasks and layout stay as they are; the world is replaced.

Constraints: fan project, never official-looking (no FFG logo, ornament art or imagery copied from arkhamhorror.com); card scans, tokens and icons unchanged; Teutonic stays on card, investigator and scenario names; Playable Green stays the legal-move ring; reduced motion; RU and EN; desktop and phone landscape.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Arkham at night, seen through a 1920s case file: a cold teal dark where the cards are the only warm things, ironwork hairlines instead of brass props. Refuses the generic dark-fantasy game HUD (leather, gold, glowing runes) and the SaaS dark theme.

OWN-WORLD: night-teal ground (#0d1317 to #122428 baize), bone ink (#f1efe4), fog-grey secondary text; one muted teal accent for chrome (chosen, current, focus); Playable Green only for legal moves, lamp gold only for the current step. Prata uppercase display over EB Garamond. Thin bone ironwork rules with flourished ends; paper notes (warm off-white, clipped) hold what you would write down.

STORY: players read the round, the prompt and their options at a glance, act on the cards, and between scenarios handle the case file: notes, photographs, stamped tags.

FIRST VIEWPORT: table: the baize with hairline frame, the top bar as a dark band with an ironwork rule, the instruction cartouche at the bottom centre over the hand fan, with the answer inside it, leather-free rail tabs at right. Title: cover art under a teal scrim, ARKHAM TABLE in Prata caps, oval ironwork buttons, the primary filled.

FORM: user-pinned direction (arkhamhorror.com-inspired noir), first on the ordered list; the roll ran afterwards for the record and the pin beats it. seed:8631e5b97f2c1cdd

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the instruction bar as an ironwork cartouche (oval with flourished end caps) that holds the current answer.
